01 / About

The short version.

I'm a biomedical engineering student interested in a deceptively simple question:

What happens when you put the human body inside a machine designed to operate at extreme speeds?

My work explores that question through engineering, biomechanics, thermal modeling, human factors, biomedical research, and motorsport.

I also write about the engineering behind racing through Behind the Visor, a technical motorsport publication focused on the bioengineering that exists underneath the headlines.

02 / Selected Work

Things I've
actually built.

PROJECT 01

Motorsport Driver Cooling

A MATLAB-based thermal management concept exploring environmental heat load, physiological inputs, phase-change materials, and cooling-system behavior for motorsport applications.

MATLAB Thermal Modeling PCM Heat Transfer
PROJECT 02

Motorsport Crash Biomechanics

Video-based analysis of motorsport crashes using kinematics, engineering mechanics, motion analysis, and force/torque concepts to investigate how drivers interact with extreme deceleration events.

Kinovea Biomechanics Kinematics Mechanics
PROJECT 03

FSAE Driver Ergonomics

Exploring driver anthropometrics, human factors, steering-wheel design, material selection, and the relationship between vehicle design and the person operating it.

FSAE Ergonomics Human Factors Engineering Design
PROJECT 04

Glovegineering: Gecko-Inspired

Exploring a potential new glove design for Formula 1 which takes inspiration from gecko feet, specifically the setae, which allows them a comfortable, easy grip. My goal is to translate this to Formula 1, without compromising the driver's potential performance.

Gloves Gecko Feet Grip Adjustment Driver Performance
