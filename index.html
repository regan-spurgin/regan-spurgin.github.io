01 / About

The short version.

I like engineering problems and I'm obsessed with the human body becoming a problem.

My work sits at the intersection of biomedical engineering, biomechanics, thermal systems, human factors, and motorsport.

Which is a slightly complicated way of saying: I'm interested in figuring out what happens to people when engineering starts getting really, really fast.

Outside of technical projects, I write about the engineering behind racing through Behind the Visor.
